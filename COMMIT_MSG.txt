Fix dubbed slug detection in ptbr.py

- Expand dubbed slug detection to also match `-dublado` anywhere in the slug,
  not just at the end, and also consider the word "dublado" in the link
  title/name. This increases the chances of correctly identifying dubbed
  content on the source site, ensuring that dubbed video streams are
  returned by the `/anime/stream` endpoint.

This fixes the issue where dubbed anime videos were not playing because
the detection logic missed slugs that contain "dublado" that aren't at the
slug end.
